# Delta 0.2.47

Your energy balance and BMI now live on Home as visual cards.

## Energy balance on Home

- A gauge for today: what you've eaten fills the bar, the deficit zone between
  your budget and maintenance is shaded, and the budget (dashed) and maintenance
  (solid) are marked. Above it: kcal left today and how far under maintenance you
  are.
- The last 7 days as deficit and surplus bars around maintenance, with each day's
  budget dashed, the week's total in kcal and body weight, and how many logged
  days were on budget.
- Tap the card for the full energy page, which now opens with the same gauge.
- Shown when the energy budget is turned on.

## BMI & weight group on Home

- Your BMI on the group scale with your target marked.
- Every group as a ladder, heaviest at the top: Obesity class III (very severe),
  class II (severe), class I (moderate), Overweight (pre-obesity), Healthy weight
  and Underweight, each with its BMI range and the weight range it means at your
  height. Your group is highlighted with your current weight.
- How far you are from the next group down and from the healthy range.
- Without a height, the card offers to add one.

Both cards appear after Trends and can be moved like any other Home card. The
energy page's BMI section shows the same ladder.
