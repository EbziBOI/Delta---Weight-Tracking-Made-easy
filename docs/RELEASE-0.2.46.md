# Delta 0.2.46

Every day now has its own calorie budget, a chart shows how far under or over
you've been, and the energy page explains your BMI and weight group with
suggestions drawn from your own numbers.

## A budget for every day

- Each day's budget adds that day's own activity from Health Connect (75% counted
  by default) instead of using one average. Today counts your usual activity until
  you beat it, so the morning budget never drops.
- Activity, steps and workouts are read back up to 90 days (with Health Connect
  history access; otherwise the 30 days it allows), so past days fill in too.
- Delta saves each day's plan while it is current, so changing your goal or
  profile later doesn't rewrite past days. Days before you set up the budget are
  estimated with the current settings and marked as such; "Recalculate past days"
  on the energy page redoes them all.
- The Diary shows a budget card for past days as well: that day's budget,
  maintenance, activity, and how far under or over you finished. With the
  automatic energy target on, each day is judged against its own budget.

## Balance chart (Diary → energy card)

- Bars show each day against its own maintenance, with that day's budget dashed:
  green on budget, orange over budget but still in deficit, yellow over
  maintenance. Partly logged days are dashed stubs and left out of the totals.
- Running total view: the deficit adds up over the range, in kcal and kg, next to
  where you'd be if every day were on budget, and next to the scale trend.
- 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month or 3 months. Tap or slide across to inspect a day:
  eaten, budget, maintenance, activity, steps.
- Average against budget, days on budget, and the current on-budget streak.

## BMI and weight group

- BMI on a scale through the WHO groups (underweight to obesity class III), with
  your target marked, what the group generally means, the healthy weight range for
  your height, how far you are from it, the next milestone and a first-5% goal.
- With a recent body-fat reading: its group for your sex, lean mass and fat mass.
- Notes on BMI's limits for muscular people, over-65s, under-18s, and the lower
  risk thresholds for some ethnic backgrounds.
- No deficit is planned while BMI is under 18.5, and a target below the healthy
  range is flagged.

## Suggestions

Up to six, from the last two weeks: logging whole days, running over budget (and
how much a gentler pace would add back), weekend eating, very low days, protein
per kg (based on a BMI-25 weight when BMI is 30 or more), fibre, steps, strength
training, and when the scale trend and your logs disagree.
