# Delta 0.2.50

Advanced search, a calmer diary, and a calorie chart that scales to fit everything.

## Advanced search

- New **Advanced** tile on Add food (and under "Not listed?" in results). Paste a
  product or recipe **link** from any website, the **nutrition table copied as text**,
  or a **product name** to look up. Delta says what it took your paste to be.
- Links are read in the background: the page loads off-screen, its nutrition table
  or recipe data is extracted and converted to per 100 g/ml for review. Tesco product
  pages, BBC Good Food and other recipe sites work; Open Food Facts links open the
  product's database record.
- Pasting a link straight into the search bar offers "Read nutrition from …".
- If a site blocks reading, **Open page and import** loads it for you, or copy its
  table and paste it.
- Recipes give values per serving: Delta shows them and asks what a serving weighs.
- Fixed: supermarket tables with a reference intake (RI/GDA) column, tables in
  collapsed sections, "per piece (12.5g)" headings and common recipe-site data
  previously imported no values.

## Diary

- Meals now come straight after the daily summary.
- An empty meal shows the food you usually have in it, with one tap to add it at
  your usual amount (replaces the separate Repeat card).
- "Other" only appears once something is logged in it.
- Hold a diary entry for a tidier menu with icons; only Delete is shown in red.

## Charts

- The calorie chart scales to fit every day. A dashed line shows your average for the
  chosen period (7D, 1M, …), and days above it are marked with an upward triangle.
  Today is left out of the average until it's over.

## Elsewhere

- Fasting: pick the start time with a clock instead of typing it; past fasts are a
  compact list.
- Targets: when your calorie budget sets the daily energy target, Targets says so.
- Recipes: remove an ingredient with its × button.
- Widgets: short, wide food widgets use a one-row layout; the wide progress
  complication adds this week's change; Ribbon and Mosaic designs draw cleanly.
