# Delta 0.2.51

Servings for every food you create.

- Every way of creating a food now has a **Serving** section: what it's called
  (Piece, Bar, Slice, Bowl, Glass… or your own word), what one weighs, and the
  whole pack. This applies to Advanced search (web pages, pasted text, label scans),
  Custom food and Recipes (name the servings: "makes 8 slices").
- It fills itself in from the source: "Per piece (12.5g)" on a shop page, a recipe's
  serving weight, or the serving on a scanned label. Delta says where it came
  from, and you can change it.
- When logging, the portion page offers the serving by name: "Number of pieces",
  "1 piece = 13 g", alongside grams and the pack.
- Fixed: the label section's photo button no longer cuts off its text.
