# Delta 0.2.44

A reworked Diary and Add food flow: clearer daily totals, tappable breakdowns,
smarter repeats, a bottom search bar, UK fast-food menus and a better scanner.

## Diary

- Protein, carbs and fat now sit beside the kcal total, each with its target.
- Tap a day on the seven-day macro chart to open that day everywhere: the kcal
  headline, macros and meals all follow. Swipe the chart to move a week, or the
  summary card to move a day. "Jump to today" returns from other dates.
- "Nutritional breakdown" expands inside the same card to show the selected
  day's macro split as a donut.
- Every breakdown donut, in the Diary and in Nutrition trends, is tappable:
  choose a segment to see a bar chart of the foods that supplied it, with
  grams, kcal and share.
- Repeat suggests only foods you have had at that meal before (breakfast foods
  for breakfast, lunch for lunch), ranked by how often and how recently you ate
  them, and skips foods already logged in that meal today. Swipe to change meal.
  The meal now starts from the time of day.
- Meals show their protein, carbs and fat. Swipe a food left to delete it, with
  Undo. Shortcut buttons gained icons and the Nutrients list collapses.

## Adding food

- Search and the barcode scanner live in a persistent bar at the bottom, kept
  above the keyboard while typing.
- Recent, My foods, Recipes and Fast food tabs swipe sideways. Creating a custom
  food, recipe, photo estimate or import is one compact row.
- Typing instantly matches your own foods and fast-food menus; press search for
  supermarket products, with store filters. A "+" on each result adds one
  portion straight to the meal, with Undo.
- Portions show live kcal and macros, with quick amount chips.

## Fast food

- Built-in UK menus for McDonald's, KFC, Burger King, Subway, Greggs, Nando's,
  Domino's, Five Guys, Starbucks and Costa, available offline.
- Values are approximate, from the chains' published UK nutrition information.
  Each item links to the chain's official guide, and "Correct values" saves an
  editable copy.

## Barcode scanning

- Full-screen camera with a viewfinder, flashlight, 2× zoom, tap to focus,
  number entry, and a gallery button for barcode photos.
- A product missing from the database, or listed without nutrition, now opens
  straight into label entry instead of an error. Values can be entered per
  100 g or per serving, and the food is saved so the next scan finds it.

## Everywhere

- Every selector also responds to a horizontal swipe.
- Trend lines use a centred, time-weighted fit: smoother, without lagging behind
  recent readings, and unchanged while panning.
