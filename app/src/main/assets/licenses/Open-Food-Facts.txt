Food label data: Open Food Facts contributors
https://world.openfoodfacts.org/

The Open Food Facts database is available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL):
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Individual database contents are available under the Database Contents Licence:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/

Delta identifies Open Food Facts foods in the library and diary food details,
and links each product back to its source. Community labels may be incomplete;
unknown nutrients are not inferred as zero.
